Work

Lead Engineer — Manhattan Associates   2020–2025

Manhattan Associates makes Manhattan Active® — a cloud-native SaaS suite covering Supply Chain, Warehouse Management, and Transportation Management, running on GCP. I owned end-to-end delivery of two modules: Dispatch and TMS Mobile.

The most interesting problem I worked on was latency. The Dispatch screen was loading in 15 seconds for dispatchers managing 100,000+ concurrent trips. That is not a typo. After profiling we found the culprit: every page load was hammering the PC*MILER routing API for mileage data that had not changed since last week. We built a Hazelcast cache in front of it, tuned HikariCP connection pooling, and got the load time to 2 seconds.

I also owned TMS Mobile — Ionic/Angular, Firebase for real-time GPS tracking of 50K+ daily shipments, talking to 25+ backend APIs I had also written. About 1,000 drivers use it daily. When I started that project I had never shipped a mobile app. I read the docs and figured it out.

Beyond those two modules: I developed and maintained shipment processing and tracking message pipelines on RabbitMQ, managed Elasticsearch for shipment search handling 1M+ daily queries, managed the EDI pipeline (X12 204/214/210/997) processing 500K+ monthly transactions, and worked with the platform audit framework to meet US DOT transaction trail requirements. I also built CI/CD pipelines in Jenkins for both Dispatch and TMS Mobile. On the side I supported the legacy Supply Chain Planning Platform — tickets, development, regression fixes, production issues. I participated in on-call rotation for 50+ enterprise clients.

A few years in I started leading the Dispatch team, doing architecture reviews, and sitting in on hiring panels. I left in 2025 to look for something new.
